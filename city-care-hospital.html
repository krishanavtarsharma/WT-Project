About the hospital

A multi-department facility built around one idea: healthcare should feel unhurried, even when it isn't.

  • Experienced doctors across five departments
  • Trained nursing and support staff
  • Modern diagnostic and treatment facilities

Modern facilities, familiar faces

City Care Hospital was built to close the gap between advanced medicine and genuine attention. Our teams work across cardiology, neurology, orthopedics, general medicine, and pediatrics, with equipment and protocols kept current so decisions aren't slowed down by outdated tools.

Whether it's a routine check-up or something that can't wait until Monday, the same standard of care applies — clear communication, careful diagnosis, and a plan you actually understand before you leave.
