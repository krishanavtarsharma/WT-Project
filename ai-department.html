About the department

Building intelligent systems that learn, reason and perceive

The Department of Artificial Intelligence trains students in the mathematics, algorithms and engineering behind machine learning, data science and intelligent systems, preparing them for careers in AI engineering, research and emerging technologies.

Dedicated AI and GPU labs support hands-on work in model training, data pipelines, and deployment, alongside seminars, hackathons and industry-sponsored projects each year.

Graduates go on to roles in ML engineering, data science, AI research, and postgraduate study.
